According to the sources, a balanced chemical equation like this one tells us:
  • The actual result of the chemical change [2].
  • The reactants involved (Zinc and Hydrochloric acid) and the products formed (Zinc chloride and Hydrogen) [2].
  • The number of molecules/atoms of each substance taking part and formed; in this case, one atom of zinc reacts with two molecules of hydrochloric acid to give one molecule of zinc chloride and one molecule of hydrogen [1, 2].
  • The physical state of the substances, where (s) stands for solid and (aq) for a solution in water [1].
